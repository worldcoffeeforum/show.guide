About World Coffee Forum

World Coffee Forum 2026

World Coffee Forum is Asia’s leading coffee knowledge and insight platform, where coffee professionals, brands, creators and everyone who loves coffee come together to learn, share inspiration and discover new perspectives.

In 2026, the World Coffee Leaders Forum takes a new step forward as the World Coffee Forum. Setting aside the weight of “Leaders”, it grows beyond a forum for industry leaders into a more open stage where perspectives from many fields connected to coffee meet.

Since 2012, the World Coffee Forum has asked the most important questions facing coffee each year. As a global coffee knowledge-sharing platform responding to the changes of a new era, it now prepares its fifteenth edition.
